Data Science / ML Intern

We are looking for a Data Science Intern to join our small AI team.

- Work with SQL databases to extract and clean data for analysis
- Build data visualizations and dashboards to communicate findings to stakeholders

- Strong foundation in Python and data analysis libraries such as Pandas and NumPy
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts and frameworks such as scikit-learn or PyTorch
- Experience with SQL and relational databases
- Bonus: experience with React or Next.js for building internal dashboards
- Bonus: experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI
- Strong communication skills and ability to explain technical results to non-technical stakeholders
